How to calibrate

  1. Find it: press Wiggle on a card and watch which joint on the robot twitches.
  2. Name it: pick that leg and joint in the card's menu.
  3. Mark 90°: nudge with −5 −1 +1 +5 (or the slider) until the joint is in Sesame's 90° pose, then press Set as 90°. 90° is the same for every joint: the leg straight out sideways, at right angles to the body (hip), with the lower leg level, not hanging down (knee).
  4. Direction: press Test +20°. The page says which way Sesame needs that joint to go; answer whether it did. The walk uses this to mirror left and right.
  5. Limits (optional): move it as far as the leg may go each way and press Set min / Set max. The slider then stops there.
On USB power alone, moving several servos at once cuts the USB port. Everything here moves one servo at a time, except Tour.

Moves (Sesame's)

On USB power: 1–4 servos at once. More, or faster, and the USB port may cut out (replug it). On a 5 V 3 A supply you can go to 8. The walk uses your calibration: 90° marks, directions, limits.
